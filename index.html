02 — The living ID

One scan.
A whole identity.

One identity, reachable three ways: a QR + NFC tag, and — trained specifically for animals — nose-print recognition with live face tracking. Any of them opens the same verified record: guardian, location, vaccination, ABC status, medical history and recovery actions.

Tags can be lost; the animal can’t. Biometrics make the body itself the key — so owned pets and community dogs stay identifiable even without a collar.

02½ — Biometric identity

Scan the nose.
Not just the tag.

Pawwltu adds AI trained for animals — nose-print recognition and live face tracking — so the body itself becomes the key to the record. A dog’s nose print is as unique as a human fingerprint.

Point a phone, let the guide frame the muzzle, and one capture links to the same verified Living ID — tag or no tag.

Track

Face & muzzle tracking

On-device detection frames the muzzle in real time and only captures when lighting, distance and focus are right.

04 — The principle

Fix identity,
then build the ecosystem.

Pawwltu’s thesis is simple: if the animal has no identity, every later product is working on broken ground.

Give the animal a record first. Then care can coordinate, cities can measure, vets can act faster and families can find their way back.

“A name is the first data point of care.”
